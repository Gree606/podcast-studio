    How Chapter Chat works

    📖1. Read

    The agent picks the passages of your chapter to cover, spread across its beginning, middle and end.

    ✍️2. Write

    One AI call writes the whole ~2-minute episode in 3 parts: Gree the astronaut explains, Error606 the alien asks what any student would ask. Each part cites its passages.

    🔎3. Fact-check & record

    A second AI call checks every claim against the book and fixes any that aren't there. Each line is then spoken and listened back to by speech recognition; mumbled lines are re-recorded.

    🎧4. Listen & play

    Tap any line to jump there. Want a part simpler or more fun? One tap rewrites just that part. Then try the quiz!
