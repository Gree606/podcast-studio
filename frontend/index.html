    How Chapter Chat works

    📖1. Read & plan

    An AI agent explores the chapter with tools (search, read, define) and plans the episode. Every key fact must point to a passage in the book, and is checked twice.

    ✍️2. Write & polish

    It writes each part as a chat between Riya and Arjun. A strict critic scores the draft on 5 things; the writer learns from the feedback and tries again. Harder parts get more tries.

    🔎3. Fact-check & record

    Every claim in the script is checked against the book. Each line is spoken, then listened to by speech recognition; mumbled lines are re-recorded.

    🎧4. Listen & play

    Tap any line to jump there. Want a part simpler or more fun? One tap rewrites just that part. Then try the quiz!

    Grown-ups and teachers: press 🎓 Teacher view to see the agent's full reasoning trace, quality scores, compute budget, claim check, sources and every version.
