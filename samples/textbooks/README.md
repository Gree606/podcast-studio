- The English chapters are NCERT's own chapter PDFs (ncert.nic.in); their text was extracted with `scripts/make_samples.py`.
- The Hindi and Malayalam chapters are NCERT's Class 6 books (the Hindi editions and NCERT's Malayalam translations). The PDFs here are the scanned pages, downscaled; the chapter text was read with Bodhan Indic-OCR and cleaned by `scripts/import_ocr_chapters.py` (running heads, page numbers, figure captions and end-of-chapter exercises left out).
- NCERT textbooks are © NCERT and are included only for educational use in this course project.
